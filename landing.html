Questions

Straight answers

Do my customers need an app?

No. They either sign on your phone while you're standing there, or you send them a link that opens in whatever browser is already on their phone. Nothing to download, no account to make.

Can my customer see what the job costs me?

No, and that's deliberate. They see the price, the deposit and what's included. Your material costs, your labor rate and your margin never leave your side of the screen. That's enforced by the system, not by remembering to hide a column.

Is this going to replace Jobber or Housecall Pro?

No, and it isn't trying to. Those run your whole office. This does one thing: gets a job priced and signed while you're still standing in the driveway. Plenty of crews will use both.

What if I don't do concrete?

Pick your trade at setup and the line items change. Pavers, landscaping, turf and fencing are built in, and you can build your own from scratch in a few minutes. The engine doesn't care what you install — it prices by area, linear foot, volume, tons, or man-hours.

What happens if I have no signal at the job?

It keeps working. The quote saves to your phone and syncs when you get bars back. Half the jobs worth quoting are out where the signal isn't.

Can I use my own logo and company name?

Yes. Upload your logo and it goes on every quote and invoice your customer sees. Nothing on the customer-facing page says our name.
